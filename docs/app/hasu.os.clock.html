About this app

Clock — alarms with repeat days and snooze, stopwatch with laps, a timer that rings from the background, and world clocks.

🆕 What's new in v1.12

NEW: a 📖 Guide page — tap Guide at the bottom of the main page for a page that explains every button and feature of HasuClock one by one, from the alarm tab and the stopwatch to the timer, the world clock, the ring screen and the widgets. FIXED: the World tab no longer tears down and rebuilds every city row on each one-second tick — the rows are built once and only their times are re-set. FIXED: a timer that already rang no longer leaves its target saved, so a restart can never re-arm it. The Guide and About pages list all three.
