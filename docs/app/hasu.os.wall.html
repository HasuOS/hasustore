About this app

Your gallery — photos & videos with editors, live wallpapers, favourites and a recycle bin.

🆕 What's new in v2.09

NEW: View backup — inside Online backup, tap View backup to SEE everything in your backup right inside HasuWall, like Google Photos: a grid of every backed-up photo and video; tap a photo to see it full-screen, tap a video to watch it (small videos stream, big ones load, then play). Viewing saves nothing to the gallery — Save to gallery is there when you want a file back. NEW: the first time the app is opened it now asks for its photo/video and notification permissions and offers to turn auto backup ON right away, so backup starts from the first entry.
