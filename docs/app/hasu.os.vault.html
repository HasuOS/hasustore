About this app

A private vault for photos, videos & PDFs — AES-256 encryption, PIN and fingerprint lock.

🆕 What's new in v1.06

NEW: the full family design system — a Look & icons page with themes (dark AND light), icon sets and five app-icon designs, each shown as the thing itself and applied the moment it is picked; the vault page and the app pages can wear different looks, and every single icon can be given your own glyph. NEW: icon packs and themes now ARRIVE BY THEMSELVES from the new vault feed — no download tap — and a notification says what arrived. NEW: a Settings page with the whole-app English ⇄ Bengali switch, notification choices (sound, vibration, quiet hours), a typed settings backup + restore, the crash report and a reset. The settings backup honestly leaves out your PIN (its hash never leaves this phone) and the vault's contents — they stay encrypted on this phone under a key that never leaves it, so export from inside the vault to move one. NEW: a full-screen preview — see the real lock and vault screens in a theme before applying it. NEW: the app checks for its own update twice a day and installs it from its own update page. DESIGN (bare-glyph law): the launcher icon is the bare white vault dial with nothing behind it, plus four more drawn designs — Lock, Key, Fingerprint, Shield; inside, every button is a bare glyph. The About page carries the new HasuOS brand logo and the developer card with a tappable email; the Guide is rewritten in English and Bengali; the Permissions hub is rebuilt with Fingerprint unlock, Notifications, Internet, Network state, Install updates and Run in background — every switch with its real live state. UNTOUCHED: the vault itself — AES-GCM encryption, the PIN pad with its brute-force lockout, fingerprint unlock, import, viewing and export work exactly as before.
