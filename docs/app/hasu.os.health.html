About this app

Health — step counter with 7-day history, daily water tracker with a goal, and a BMI calculator. All on your phone.

🆕 What's new in v1.01

NEW: a daily step goal — set it from the Steps card (10,000 by default) and watch your progress live, with a celebration when you reach it. The BMI card now shows the healthy weight range for your height, and the About page now carries the HasuOS website row.
