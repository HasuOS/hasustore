HasuStore — the family's own store

Every HasuOS app lives in HasuStore. Install it once and it keeps the whole family updated the way the Play Store does — with real download progress, a What's new card for every update, and a notification when something new is ready.

Why a store of its own?

Because the family grows fast. HasuStore carries every app in one place, checks for updates in the background, and shows you exactly what changed before you install. No hunting for files, no stale versions.

⬆ Update allOne tap updates every installed HasuOS app to its latest version.

About Hasu

Hasu is an independent Android developer from Bangladesh who builds the software he wants to use: fast, beautiful, honest apps with no ads and no accounts. HasuOS started as a keyboard and a launcher; it is now a complete family — camera, files, music, clock, store and more — designed as one world in liquid glass, and improved a little every single day.

Every app on this page was designed, built and tested by Hasu himself, on a phone just like yours.
